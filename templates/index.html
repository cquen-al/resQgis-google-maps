Know what the map shows

ResQGIS helps you explore emergency resources and geographic accessibility in Butuan City. It is an educational GIS, not a dispatch or live hazard-warning system.

How analysis works

Nearest-facility results use Google driving routes. Coverage uses geographic buffers. Barangay accessibility measures the land area intersecting the union of those buffers; nearby counts and nearest distances use a representative interior point.

These measures do not account for road access conditions, flooding, population, capacity, or travel time. No official accessibility thresholds are implied.

Map preview circles use Turf.js while completed analyses are calculated in PostGIS.
